Laser scanning system

 RIEGL VZ-2000i

Based on RIEGL's innovative, forward-looking data processing architecture, internet connectivity and the latest LiDAR waveform processing technology, the RIEGL VZ-2000i long-range 3D laser scanning system combines ease of use in the field with fast, high-accuracy data collection.

 Innovative approach

The new processing architecture allows various background tasks (such as point cloud registration, georeferencing, orientation using the integrated inertial measurement unit, etc.) to be performed on board in parallel with the simultaneous acquisition of scan and image data.

 Advanced Features

Complete documentation of the scanner software components, available directly on the RIEGL VZ-2000i, provides a solid basis for creating your own python applications to extend the scanner's functionality.

High-speed data acquisition

 Simcenter SCADAS Lab Solution

for high-speed data acquisition in the lab
• The system is designed to fit in a standard 19-inch rack and is powered by AC power
• Channel scalability: from eight to over a thousand channels
• Excellent data quality, high throughput and channel density: up to 480 channels in a single crate
• Supports unlimited channels
• Powerful system that does not take up much space in the lab

 Multi-channel testing

The Simcenter SCADAS Lab solution offers matching amplifiers with unmatched throughput independent of the number of channels, making it an ideal choice for multi-channel modal testing, aircraft ground frequency testing, high-frequency acoustic testing, turbine testing, and other data-intensive measurements. In addition, the mobile capabilities of the SCADAS platform allow the Simcenter SCADAS Lab crate to be connected to the Simcenter SCADAS Mobile analyzer or Simcenter SCADAS Recorder without the need to reconfigure the data acquisition system.

 Technical specifications

• Sampling rate up to 204.8 kHz per channel, throughput up to 19 Msps
• 24-bit sigma-delta ADC
• 150 dB dynamic range
• Various types of matching amplifiers and a large selection of standard connectors, including BNC, CAMAC LEMO® and Sub-D for use with patch panels
• Optional interfaces for connection to digital CAN-bus data buses, two inputs for connecting speed sensors and outputs from signal generators
• Connection of "master" and "slave" crates for distributed configuration of data acquisition systems using long-distance fiber optic cables with a data transfer rate of 1.25 Gbps
• Only high-quality electronic components with an extended operating temperature range are used in the amplifiers for optimal reliability

Inclinometers

A team of specialists with the help of a high-tech complex, the fastest and most accurate inclinometry system REFLEX GYRO SPRINT-IQ, together with your company's drilling operators, will perform all continuous survey work with an electronic depth meter in real time - users get instant access to results via the IMDEXHUB-IQ™ cloud solution. When working in continuous mode, the advantages of REFLEX GYRO SPRINT-IQ™ include a survey speed of more than 150 meters per minute with constant quality and accuracy of results. With REFLEX GYRO SPRINT-IQ™, you can be sure of the survey data that comes from north-oriented sensors.  Siemens Scadas Lab

Our company implements a solution for complex laboratory testing with the Siemens system - Simcenter SCADAS Lab, for high-speed data acquisition.
Simcenter SCADAS Lab equipment offers matching amplifiers with unrivaled bandwidth, independent of the number of channels, which makes it an ideal choice for multi-channel modal testing, ground frequency testing of aircraft, high-frequency acoustic testing, turbine testing and other types of measurements that require processing large volumes of data.
In addition, the mobile properties of the SCADAS platform allow you to connect the Simcenter SCADAS Lab crate to the Simcenter SCADAS Mobile analyzer or Simcenter SCADAS Recorder without the need to reconfigure the data acquisition system.
Our engineers will conduct safe tests of expensive products, thanks to the Simcenter SCADAS Lab system, it can be used as a controller for the vibration testing control system with a hardware emergency shutdown.
We offer retrofitting with fiber optic cables as part of the system, and it is also possible to connect "slave" crates. This modular architecture is especially convenient for vibroacoustic laboratories – it is possible to build a system of several crates for more than a thousand channels. From several “master” crates it is possible to assemble a “master-slave” configuration, which will work as a single system with one control station.
